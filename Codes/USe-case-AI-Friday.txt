Here's a concise AI Friday (HR Theme) use case that works well for a TCS-style innovation showcase:

Use Case: AI-Powered Employee Pulse & Attrition Risk Assistant

Business Problem:
 HR teams spend significant time analyzing employee feedback, engagement survey results, manager comments, and exit interviews. Identifying employees at risk of disengagement or attrition is often reactive.

AI Solution:
 Use Generative AI to:

Analyze employee survey responses, feedback forms, and internal communications.
Detect sentiment trends and engagement levels.
Identify potential attrition risks based on patterns.
Generate personalized retention recommendations for managers.
Create HR dashboards with actionable insights.

Business Impact:

Early intervention for at-risk employees.
Improved employee retention.
Faster HR decision-making.
Better employee experience.
Reduced manual effort in analyzing large volumes of feedback data.

AI Technologies Involved:

Azure OpenAI / GPT
Sentiment Analysis
Natural Language Processing (NLP)
Power BI for visualization
Microsoft Copilot integration

AI Friday Demo Scenario: "An HR manager uploads quarterly employee feedback. The AI assistant summarizes key concerns, identifies departments with declining engagement, flags retention risks, and suggests targeted action plans within minutes."

This is practical, business-focused, and easy to demonstrate in a 5-minute AI Friday session.
